Character posting and message replacement

Creating a character saves its name and portrait, and publishes a persistent Discord portrait profile. /char use selects one of your characters for your ordinary messages in the current channel or thread. This selection is remembered across restarts until you switch it, remove the character, erase your data, or use /char off. Starting a message with // leaves that message unchanged as out-of-character text. /say is available for individual short character posts.

With an active character selected, you direct Nightfall to repost eligible messages and attachments under that character's name and portrait, then remove your original after successful delivery. The copy is a new webhook message. Long posts can be split, formatting can differ at boundaries, and failures or interruptions can leave duplicates. Automatic editing of copied posts is not included in this version. Review the channel after a problem and contact a moderator or the operator if a copy needs removal.

Only select character posting in channels where you want it. Server administrators control the bot's channel access and permissions. Do not use the fictional name or portrait to conceal harassment or other violations.

Game results and chronicle announcements

Dice calculations, reroll buttons, rule labels, and stored profiles are game aids and may contain errors or omissions. Players and the Storyteller remain responsible for game rulings and records. Resource changes such as Willpower costs and Hunger must be recorded on your own sheet; Nightfall does not maintain a full character-sheet tracker.

Manage Server permission is required to configure or post reset announcements. A reset is an announcement, not deletion of channels or messages. Scheduling uses Europe/Prague local time and a fourteen-day interval. The host PC and bot must be running; the latest missed scheduled post can be delivered when it resumes. External weather and astronomy data can be unavailable, delayed, or inaccurate and is supplied for chronicle context. The lunar information is labeled for local noon.
