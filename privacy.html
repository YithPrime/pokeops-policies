PokéOps Privacy Policy

Effective October 22, 2026

PokéOps is a community-made Discord bot for tabletop Pokémon games. This policy explains the information the bot may process to provide its commands.

Information processed

When you interact with the bot, it processes the information necessary to execute your command. This may include Discord user IDs, Discord server IDs, command arguments, dice results, and Pokémon or character information that users enter into the bot.

Storage and purpose

PokéOps may save user-provided Pokémon roster and gameplay data, associated with Discord identifiers, in a database so that game-related commands work between sessions. Information is used to operate and maintain the bot, not to sell personal data or serve advertisements.

Sharing

Gameplay output may be visible to people in the Discord channel where a command is used. Discord separately processes interactions under its own policies. PokéOps does not intentionally sell user data to advertisers.

Retention and deletion

Stored gameplay data is retained while needed for the bot's operation or until it is deleted. Users may request deletion of their stored bot data through the bot's /privacy_erase command, if available in their installed version. For assistance or unresolved deletion requests, contact the bot operator using the support contact below.

Security

Reasonable efforts are made to limit access to stored data, but no service can guarantee absolute security. Please do not submit sensitive personal information through game commands.

Children

PokéOps is intended for people eligible to use Discord under Discord's terms. It is not designed to collect information from children below Discord's minimum age.

Changes

This policy may be updated as PokéOps changes. The effective date above will be revised when material changes are made.

Contact

For privacy questions, deletion assistance, or other support, contact the PokéOps operator through the PokéOps support page. Issues are public; do not include sensitive personal information.
